Your Privacy Matters

Tonight is designed to help a household manage a shared TV watch list. Your household data is stored on your devices and synced with Apple's iCloud services. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising or tracking.

Information You Add to Tonight

Tonight stores the shows, streaming services, season progress, watched-by choices, household display names, and related planner details that you enter or select in the app.

This information is used only to provide the app's core functionality: showing your shared planner, syncing changes across devices, and helping your household decide what to watch.

Sign in with Apple

Tonight uses Sign in with Apple so the app can identify the current app user and associate that user with a household profile. The app may store your Apple user identifier, display name, and authentication token on your device. If you choose a household display name, that name may be visible to members of your shared household.

iCloud Sync and Sharing

Tonight uses Apple's CloudKit and Core Data with iCloud to sync your household planner across your devices and with household members you invite. Shared household data is stored in iCloud under Apple's CloudKit infrastructure and is controlled by the iCloud accounts participating in the share.

We do not operate a separate database for your household planner data.

Show Search and Artwork

When you search for a show or load show details, Tonight contacts The Movie Database (TMDB) to find show metadata, artwork, seasons, and streaming-provider information. Search terms and show identifiers may be sent to TMDB to complete those requests.

TMDB is a third-party service. Its handling of requests is governed by its own policies.

AI Features

Tonight may include optional AI-assisted features in future releases, such as helping choose a show or checking show status. When those features are used, selected show details needed to answer the request may be sent to Tonight's app service and to an AI provider. These features are used to provide the requested app functionality and are not used for advertising or tracking.

Data We Do Not Collect

Tonight does not collect or use data for third-party advertising, ad measurement, or cross-app tracking.

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not collect precise location.
  • We do not collect contacts, photos, microphones, or camera data.
  • We do not include third-party advertising SDKs.
  • We do not use third-party analytics SDKs.

Apple TV

The Apple TV version of Tonight reads the same iCloud-synced household planner for the signed-in iCloud account. It is intended for viewing and updating existing planner items from Apple TV.

Data Deletion

You can remove shows, archive shows, leave a shared household, or delete the app from your devices. Household data synced through iCloud can also be managed through your iCloud account and the devices participating in the share.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the date below and post the new policy on this page.

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please visit our support page.
