Frequently Asked Questions

How do I share my household?

On iPhone, open Settings in CouchQueue and tap Invite Partner. Send the iCloud share link to the person you want to invite. They should install CouchQueue, sign in with Apple, and open the invite link on their device.

Why are shows not syncing?

Make sure each device is signed into iCloud, iCloud Drive is enabled, and the invited account has accepted the household share. TestFlight and App Store builds use the production CloudKit environment.

Can I add shows on Apple TV?

The Apple TV app is focused on viewing and updating shows already in your planner. Add and organize shows from the iPhone app, then Apple TV will sync them through iCloud.

Why does opening a streaming app not go directly to the show?

Streaming apps vary in how much deep linking they support. CouchQueue opens the streaming app when possible and may show a fallback instruction when a direct link is not available.

How do I remove a show?

Open a show from the iPhone app and use Archive to remove it from your active planner.
