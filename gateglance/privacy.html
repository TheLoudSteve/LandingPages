PRIVACY POLICY

Travel information should stay yours.

Gate Glance is designed to show flight details with the minimum data needed to do so.

Calendar access

With your permission, Gate Glance reads upcoming events from Apple Calendars whose names contain “TripIt” to identify scheduled flights. The app does not create, edit, delete, or share calendar events.

On-device storage and Apple Watch

The app stores its flight roster, notification journal, and widget snapshot locally on your iPhone. If you use the Apple Watch app or complication, it sends a local flight snapshot to your paired watch using Apple’s WatchConnectivity framework. The watch stores that snapshot locally.

Shortcut input

If you choose to configure a Shortcuts automation, Gate Glance stores only the notification text explicitly supplied by that automation and the source label you configure. It does not independently read your notifications or notification history.

Optional remote flight updates

Some pre-release or privately configured versions can retrieve flight-update text from a private Gate Glance service. When enabled by you, forwarded flight-update emails are received and processed to provide those updates. Raw email is retained for up to 14 days; the derived update text is retained for up to 30 days. This service is not used unless it is configured for your copy of the app.

No tracking or advertising

Gate Glance does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking, or data brokers. We do not sell personal information.

Data deletion

You can remove locally stored Gate Glance data by deleting the app. For optional remote updates, contact support to request deletion of service-held data.

Contact

For privacy questions, visit the support page.
