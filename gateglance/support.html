Help with Gate Glance.

A few quick checks usually get your flight back in view.

Set up your flights

  1. Keep your TripIt itinerary in an Apple Calendar whose name contains “TripIt.”
  2. Open Gate Glance and grant calendar access when prompted.
  3. Use Import TripIt flights to refresh the upcoming-flight list.

Widget or watch looks stale

Open Gate Glance on your iPhone. The app refreshes its local widget and watch snapshot when it becomes active. Confirm that the watch is paired and that the Gate Glance watch app is installed.

Gate information is missing or incorrect

Gate assignments are supplied by the flight-information source and can change at any time. Always confirm the gate and boarding information with the airline or airport before traveling to it.

Contact

For help, feature requests, or privacy questions, open an issue on the Gate Glance project.
