Your Privacy Matters

Calendar Clarity is designed as a local-first calendar presentation tool. Calendar data is read and processed on your device so the app can show a clearer agenda in the app, widgets, and Apple Watch complications.

Calendar Access

Calendar Clarity requests access to your calendars to:

  • List calendars available on your device
  • Read upcoming events for your effective agenda
  • Apply local rules that hide placeholder or lower-priority events
  • Create local snapshots for widgets and Apple Watch complications

The app does not create, edit, delete, or modify your calendar events.

Local Storage

Calendar Clarity stores your calendar priority, hidden-event rules, and widget snapshots locally on your device. The iPhone app and iOS widgets share this data through Apple's App Group storage.

Apple Watch

If you use the Apple Watch app or complication, the iPhone app may send a local agenda snapshot to your paired Apple Watch using Apple's WatchConnectivity framework. The watch stores that snapshot locally so the complication can display your next event.

No Account, Server, Analytics, or Tracking

Calendar Clarity does not require an account and does not operate a backend service. We do not collect analytics, track you, sell your data, or use your data for advertising.

Data We Do Not Collect

Calendar Clarity does not collect:

  • Your calendar events
  • Your calendar account details
  • Your location
  • Usage analytics
  • Device identifiers
  • Contact, photo, microphone, or camera data

Third-Party Sharing

We do not share calendar data with third parties because Calendar Clarity does not collect or transmit calendar data to us.

Data Deletion

You can remove Calendar Clarity's locally stored settings and snapshots by deleting the app from your devices. Calendar events remain in your original calendar accounts and are not modified by Calendar Clarity.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the date below and post the new policy on this page.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please visit our support page.
