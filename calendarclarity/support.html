Support

Calendar Clarity helps you create a cleaner agenda from the calendars already on your device.

Common Setup Steps

  • Grant calendar access when prompted.
  • Open the Calendars tab and drag calendars into priority order.
  • Drag calendars below the ignored divider if they should never appear.
  • Create hidden-event rules for placeholder titles that should not appear in widgets or complications.
  • Open the app or tap Refresh Widgets after changing rules.

Widgets and Apple Watch

Widgets and complications use a cached snapshot from the iPhone app. If a widget or complication looks stale, open Calendar Clarity on your iPhone and refresh widgets from Settings.
